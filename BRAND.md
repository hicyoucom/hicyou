# HiCyou brand guidance

The Apache License 2.0 grants rights to the software but does not grant rights to use HiCyou trademarks except as required for reasonable description of the software's origin.

You may use the unmodified “Powered by HiCyou” badge to state that a deployment is based on this project, provided the use does not suggest sponsorship, certification, or endorsement. Link the badge to <https://hicyou.com> or <https://github.com/hicyoucom/hicyou>.

Keeping the badge is appreciated but completely voluntary. Removing, replacing, or declining to display it does not affect your rights to use, modify, or distribute the software under Apache-2.0.

Forks should use a distinct product name and visual identity when their changes could reasonably be confused with the official HiCyou service.
